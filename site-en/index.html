      How to read this data

      • Usage: The percentage of teams containing that Pokémon (monthly Smogon statistics, rating or above). Lists and trends combine Mega Evolutions with their base forms.
      • Effective number of Pokémon: The effective number of Pokémon after accounting for uneven usage (Hill number q=1). Higher means more diversity.
      • Turnover: How much the usage distribution changed from the previous month (0% means unchanged).
      • Cores: Groups of Pokémon often used on the same team.
      • Bring rate: The share of team-preview appearances that resulted in entering battle. Based only on public Showdown replays, so it is indicative only.
      • Dotted lines mark regulation changes. Lines are not connected across different metagames.
      • These statistics cover battles on Pokémon Showdown, not in-game ranked battles.
